The Notice · Name · Navigate method

Three steps drawn from mindfulness-based, cognitive behavioral and DBT skills work, turned into a routine you can actually keep.

Notice

Catch it early

Learn to spot the first signals of stress in your body and thoughts, before they snowball into a spiral.

Name

Make sense of it

Put words to what you feel. Naming an emotion turns down its volume and gives you room to choose.

Navigate

Respond, don't react

Use grounding, reframing and self-compassion skills to move through hard moments with steadiness.

Questions

Is this therapy?

No. These are educational programs that teach evidence-informed coping and mindfulness skills. They don't diagnose or treat any condition. If you're looking for therapy, I'm happy to point you toward licensed providers.

Who is this for?

Students, young professionals and anyone dealing with everyday stress, overthinking, burnout or a life transition who wants practical tools they can use right away.

Do I need any experience with meditation?

None at all. Every practice is short, guided and explained in plain language. If you can breathe, you can start.

What if I can't make a live session?

Every session of The Mindful Reset is recorded, and the community stays open between calls so you never fall behind.

What if I'm in crisis?

These programs aren't designed for crisis support. In the US, call or text 988 any time, or text HOME to 741741 to reach Crisis Text Line. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.
